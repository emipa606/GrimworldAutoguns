Hey there fellow scavs and scabs. 

I have another selection of equipment from the 41st millennium here for you all, a collection of autoguns and stub guns beloved by guardsmen and hive gangers alike.


Details:

Adds 8 new firearms inspired by Warhammer 40k, craftable by progressing through the standard firearm research trees.

Currently Included are the Agripinaa MKII Autogun and its tanker variant, a Heavy Stubber, a Militarum Shotgun/Sawn Off Shotgun, an Orlock Autogun, Hiveworld Autopistol and the Zarona MKII Stub Revolver.

All textures are 512 x 512


Recommended mods:

Graphics Settings+

Camera+
